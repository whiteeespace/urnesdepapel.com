SUZANNE DESBIENS
Having completed a professional career as a visual artist, her research led her to explore different artisanal techniques of paper making and weaving.
Over time, she developed an interest in making paper objects from a variety of plant fibers as well as of scraps of silk, cotton and linen fabrics.  The idea of designing and making paper funeral urns emerged. Upon the death of a loved one, she was commissioned to make her first urns out of paper.  
It is truly this experience that gave birth to URNES DE PAPEL. Freed from the taboos surrounding death, Suzanne Desbiens wanted to share her humanistic and eco-responsible values as well as her know-how with the bereaved through her urns.  She considers herself privileged to make the receptacle of deceased people’s ashes, their final resting place, in a way. 
Suzanne Desbiens is a member of the IAPMA, International Association of Paper Maker Artists.
FUNERAL URNS BIODEGRADABLE HANDMADE
Founded in 2013 by Suzanne Desbiens, professional visual artist, 
URNES DE PAPEL’s mission is to create and handmade manufacture biodegradable funeral urns with art paper. 
OUR MISSION
We want to make our urns accessible to people who share our values: empathy, respect, creativity and know-how.  We are committed to creating and making original objects from methods and raw materials that respect our environment.
OUR URNS
Our urns are handcrafted and are all unique pieces. Made with an organic raw material, they have a textured and irregular surface, the result of the living quality of the material that during the drying process reacts to the elements of the environment. Unlike machined and smooth products, these small and unexpected reliefs give them a natural and human aspect.  The finishes are made by adding embossed papers, elements of nature or raw fibers.  Our urns are shaped from robust fibres such as abaca and kozo and completed with fibers from our territory such as flax, hemp and milkweed.  Our glues, varnishes and pigments are natural and non-toxic.   
PERSONALIZED URNS
On special request, various elements can be integrated into the urn at the time of its manufacture or when finishing the urn.  Fragments of clothing that belonged to the deceased loved one, a mother-of-pearl button, the page of a book dear to the deceased or photos. Our urns degrade in soil or water without leaving toxic waste.
